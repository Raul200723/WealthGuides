Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This explains what cookies WealthGuides uses and why.

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function and, in some cases, help advertisers show relevant ads.

Types of cookies we use

TypePurposeCan you opt out?
EssentialCore site functionality (e.g., remembering menu state)Not required for site to function without them, but disabling may reduce functionality
AnalyticsUnderstand aggregate site usage to improve contentYes, via browser settings or consent tool
AdvertisingGoogle AdSense and partners may use cookies to serve relevant adsYes, via Google Ads Settings

Managing cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies via settings. Blocking all cookies may affect site functionality. For advertising cookies specifically, visit aboutads.info or Google Ads Settings.

Changes

We may update this policy as our use of cookies changes. Check back periodically for updates.
