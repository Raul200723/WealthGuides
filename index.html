Frequently asked questions

Is WealthGuides financial advice?

No. WealthGuides is an educational publication. Content is for informational purposes only and is not personalized investment, tax, or legal advice. See our full disclaimer.

How is WealthGuides funded?

WealthGuides is supported by display advertising and, in some cases, affiliate partnerships disclosed on the relevant page. Advertising and affiliate relationships never influence which funds or brokers we cover or how we describe them — see our methodology.

How often is content updated?

Fee, return, and account-limit figures are checked on a quarterly cycle at minimum. Every article displays its last-updated date, and material changes are logged in a visible corrections note at the bottom of the article.
