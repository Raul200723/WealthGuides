Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information WealthGuides ("we," "us") collects when you use wealthguides.online, why, and what choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you provide

If you subscribe to our newsletter or use the contact form, we collect the information you enter (typically name and email address).

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we use analytics and advertising technology that may collect: IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, referring site, and approximate location derived from IP address. This is collected via cookies and similar technologies — see our Cookie Policy for detail.

How we use information

  • To operate and improve the website (understanding which content is useful).
  • To send newsletter emails to subscribers who opted in, and nothing else.
  • To respond to contact form submissions.
  • To serve advertising, including via Google AdSense (see below).

Google AdSense & advertising

We use or intend to use Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or generally at aboutads.info.

Analytics

We use web analytics tools to understand aggregate site usage. Analytics data is used in aggregate and is not used to identify individual visitors.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion of personal information, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (which, under California law, can include certain advertising-cookie data sharing). To exercise these rights, contact hello@wealthguides.online. We do not sell personal information for monetary payment.

European users (GDPR)

If you access this site from the European Economic Area or UK, our legal basis for processing is generally consent (for newsletter signup and non-essential cookies) or legitimate interest (for essential site operation). You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

Data retention

Newsletter subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe. Contact form submissions are retained as needed to respond and for a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping.

Children's privacy

WealthGuides is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@wealthguides.online.
